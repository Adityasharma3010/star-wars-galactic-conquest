# Commander rules

Two to eight commanders start with 1,500 credits by default. All begin at Hyperspace. Characters and faction selections have no economic advantage. The initial player list fixes turn order before play.

Roll two six-sided dice and move clockwise. Passing Hyperspace earns 200 credits. Doubles earn an extra roll after resolving the landing; three consecutive doubles send the commander to detention. Online dice and card shuffles are generated by the trusted server.

Buy an unclaimed property at its listed price or decline. With auctions enabled, every active commander may bid, including the commander who declined. Bids must increase by at least 10 credits and be affordable. Passing is final. A bid renews the 30-second countdown. After timeout, non-leading commanders pass automatically; the remaining leader pays and receives the property.

There are 22 planets, four transports and two utilities. Undeveloped rent doubles for a complete color group. Transport rent is 25/50/100/200 depending on ownership; utility rent is 4× or 10× the dice total. Mortgaged assets collect no rent. The full deed lists rent at every development level.

Own a full group before developing it. Develop evenly, up to four outposts, then a planetary base. The default bank holds 32 outposts and 12 bases. Sell developments evenly for half their cost. Downgrading a base requires four available outposts. Sell all group developments before mortgaging or trading its planets.

A mortgage pays the listed value. Redeeming costs principal plus 10% interest, rounded upward. A recipient of a mortgaged property in a trade pays its transfer interest. Offers exchange properties, credits and retained escape cards; all assets and balances are revalidated on acceptance. Counteroffers replace an existing offer from the recipient's perspective. Offers expire after three rounds.

Detention can be escaped by paying 50 credits before a roll, using a retained escape card, or rolling doubles. Escape doubles do not earn another roll. After three failed rolls, the fine must be paid before moving with the third roll.

Force and Galactic decks contain 24 original, functional events: credits, payments to/from others, repairs, movement, nearest transport/utility, detention and escape. Cards draw without replacement; retained escape cards leave the deck until used or returned to the bank.

Taxes default to 200 and 100 credits. With the Free Orbit house rule, bank payments accumulate in its pool. Otherwise Free Orbit is a rest space.

Payments never leave a negative wallet. A shortfall opens debt settlement. Sell developments and mortgage eligible worlds, then settle the remaining debt. Bankruptcy transfers assets to the creditor or returns them to the bank, eliminates the debtor, and checks victory. The last commander or team standing wins. Team mode supports no teammate rent and optional shared ownership for group development.

Ordinary turns have no countdown. Online sessions send heartbeats every 12 seconds. After 30 seconds without a heartbeat, a commander receives five full minutes to reconnect; using Leave match reserves the seat for two minutes. Multiple live tabs count as one connected commander. After grace expires, temporary AI makes legal decisions for that seat. Reconnecting restores human control and cancels pending automatic actions. Closing a tab sends a best-effort leave signal; missing signals use the disconnect grace. An entirely offline match remains saved until a client returns.

An auction starts with 30 seconds for the first bid. Every accepted bid resets its closing countdown to 10 seconds. All other commanders passing closes it immediately. Late bids are rejected by the trusted engine.

Force and Galactic Events open animated mission briefings. Dismiss the presentation with Continue mission after reading it. The card effect is already committed, so dismissing or reopening the briefing cannot apply it twice.

Custom rules include credits, starting bonus, taxes, auctions, trading, bank inventory, Free Orbit, team options, duplicate characters and AI difficulty. Quick mode starts with 1,000 credits and ends after 20 rounds; the richest commander or team wins. Custom games can set a different round limit, or zero for classic elimination victory. Mortgages can be disabled. AI uses legal dice and follows the same rules as human commanders.
