# India map

Choose **Default → India** on the map screen, then play locally or create an online room. India has 40 spaces and the same property-trading rules as Galactic Conquest. Currency is rupees (₹). Pick one of eight colored pill tokens; factions and the Star Wars intro are absent.

| Color group             | Capital cities                         |
| ----------------------- | -------------------------------------- |
| Hill Capitals           | Shimla, Gangtok                        |
| Northeast Capitals      | Shillong, Kohima, Itanagar             |
| Eastern Capitals        | Patna, Ranchi, Bhubaneswar             |
| Heartland Capitals      | Bhopal, Raipur, Lucknow                |
| Western Capitals        | Panaji, Gandhinagar, Jaipur            |
| Southern Capitals       | Thiruvananthapuram, Bengaluru, Chennai |
| Metropolitan Capitals   | Hyderabad, Kolkata, Mumbai             |
| Administrative Capitals | Chandigarh, New Delhi                  |

Property photos are real Wikimedia Commons photographs. Shimla's photo depicts the city; Gangtok's depicts M.G. Marg; the New Delhi poster depicts Rashtrapati Bhavan. Each file's author, source and individual license is documented in [photo credits](public/images/india/CREDITS.md) and the adjacent manifest. The map includes a selection of Indian capitals, rather than every state and union territory.

The other spaces are GO, Jail, Free Parking, Go to Jail, four regional railways, Electric Company, Water Works, Income Tax, Luxury Tax, three Chance and three Community Chest spaces. Houses and hotels appear above owned properties. Owner-colored borders and pill markers identify holdings; hover a player's card to find their location, or click it to filter their properties.

Income Tax always charges **10% of cash** by default. Custom Rules can choose **10% of total wealth**; fractional tax rounds upward. Luxury Tax is separately configurable. India uses its own original dice/card/coin effects and a quiet original instrumental, **City Evening**, generated by `scripts/generate-classic-music.js`. Personal background songs still remain on the user's device.

Online visitors who arrive after match start can spectate. Existing players have five minutes after disconnect detection or two minutes after leaving to rejoin. Expired human seats are eliminated without replacement bots. A sole survivor wins; all-player expiration ends without a winner. Join next match opens the following waiting room after completion.
