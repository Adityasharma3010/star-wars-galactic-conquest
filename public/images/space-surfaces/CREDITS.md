# Planet surface materials

Equirectangular textures by **Solar System Scope / INOVE**, [source collection](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/), used under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). These are adapted NASA-based solar-system materials, colorized and combined with original procedural masks for fictional game worlds. They are not official Star Wars planet maps.
