# Built-in Star Wars sound effects

Creator-made recreations of familiar Star Wars sounds. No RichUp files or film soundtrack samples.

- **Enhanced blaster** by MikeE63. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). [Original source](https://freesound.org/people/MikeE63/sounds/466834/). The public high-quality preview is bundled; excerpts are played for UI actions.
- **R2-D2-style chirp** by Ashe Kirk / OwlStorm / Owlish Media. [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). [Original source](https://freesound.org/people/OwlStorm/sounds/404759/). The public high-quality preview is bundled; excerpts are played for UI actions.
- **Lightsaber recreation** by Bruna Mattos, João Gilberto Tenório, Taiane Ketily / posdesigndesom. [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Original source](https://freesound.org/people/posdesigndesom/sounds/848168/). The public high-quality preview is bundled; excerpts are played for UI actions.
